## Let Builders Build

Managing a large-scale construction project, from design to closeout, is complex and data-intensive.

General contractors (GCs) and their trade partners navigate ever-changing documentation, relying on fragmented digital tools and multi-party workflows to extract critical information. This document hunt consumes a large portion of the workday for Project Managers and Superintendents.

The Trunk Tools platform reclaims that lost time, allowing builders to focus on what they do best: building.

## The Brain Behind Construction

Knowledge of this massive document set is already distributed across a wide range of specialists who work both on and off the jobsite. Utilizing construction-centric AI, backed by veteran industry professionals, we organize vast amounts of unstructured data, automate repetitive workflows, and significantly boost field productivity.

Trunk Tools puts all this knowledge in one place: one giant construction brain dedicated to your workflows, your documents, your project.

## Workflow Automation Agents

Construction involves numerous repetitive administrative tasks, from compiling weekly meeting information to managing procurement timelines, and analyzing bids. ## TrunkSubmittal

## Discrepancy Detection Agent

TrunkSubmittal helps construction teams catch discrepancies across project documentation – starting with spec and submittal reviews – before they turn into schedule delays, RFIs, or rework. Powered by AI and tailored to the realities of construction workflows, it flags missing, conflicting, and noncompliant information early — giving your team the clarity they need to move faster and build with confidence.

Built within the Trunk Tools platform and trusted by leading contractors, TrunkSubmittal fits naturally into your processes, accelerating reviews and strengthening project delivery without adding extra overhead.

[Learn More](https://detect.trunktools.com/)

## Submittal Register

TrunkSubmittal Register extracts every requirement from your spec book, builds a complete product hierarchy, and publishes directly to your project management system, so your team starts every project with a complete, accurate submittal log from day one.

## TrunkReview

## Drawing Revision Agent

TrunkReview accelerates the process of interpreting changes in drawing revisions and helps your team avoid missing details that can snowball into $10,000+ rework events.

Leveraging cutting-edge vision language models trained by architects and construction industry pros, the agent scans new revisions in just a few minutes, identifies both clouded and unclouded changes, and produces a visual overlay and bullet point list of changes with links to associated sheets. With this data, you can more quickly determine the impact of the changes and take action.

[Learn More](https://trunktools.com/trunkreview)

## TrunkText

## Q&A Agent

Built on top of the Trunk Tools platform, TrunkText gives your team instant access to everything you need to know for anything you need to build.

It connects directly to your preferred software, then reads, parses, and structures all the documents, drawings, schedules, RFIs, submittals, procurement logs, bids, contracts, and meeting minutes into searchable data. Field workers can ask TrunkText questions and get accurate answers backed by documentation in seconds.

Unlike broader AI tools, TrunkText is laser-focused on construction and trained specifically on your project’s data, offering a chat-based interface to access information quickly.
