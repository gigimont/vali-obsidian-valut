ConnHack 2018 - 1st Place Team

Issued by SAP, VattenFall, FRESH Energy, Conrad and RWE · May 2018

Associated with Data Science Retreat

Our team of five people designed a "VoltKraft" solution for grid operators. Grid operators can use "VoltKraft" dashboard not only to monitor, purchase, or sell power, but also to shape the demand by pushing incentives to consumers. Consumers can choose to receive push notifications with special discounts or offers ("VoltCoins") to adjust their behavior (connect E-car for storage, keep appliances turned off, or sell PV to grid, etc.). The "VoltKraft" solution was based on a forecasting LSTM model of grid overages or shortages and Google Cloud Analytics (Text is modified based on Marcus Jones’s summary). http://connhack.org
