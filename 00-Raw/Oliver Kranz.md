## About

Oliver is an accomplished entrepreneur with extensive experience in the aviation and aerospace industry. He holds a PhD from TU Berlin and co-founded PACE Aerospace Engineering and Information Technology GmbH, successfully transforming it into a global leader in digitalization and decarbonization. Since his exit, Oliver has been active as a Business Angel, supporting software and hardware startups in the B2B sector. He focuses on fostering innovation, guiding strategic growth, and advancing technology and sustainability.
