## About

Over the past 15+ years, I’ve worked across startups, consultancies and corporates, building more than 20 ventures across IoT, mobility, energy and industrial systems. This includes corporate spin-offs, hardware products and companies that reached exit. Today, I lead global investment strategy for Physical AI, Industrial IoT and Robotics at Niterra Ventures, the corporate VC arm of a Japanese Tier 1 automotive supplier. My role is to decide where to place capital and focus, and which technologies are worth scaling into real-world systems. I operate at the intersection of: - early-stage building and uncertainty - corporate execution and scale - hardware and software product systems I’ve worked across Germany, Japan and the US, and regularly align founders, engineers and corporate stakeholders to move from idea to execution. Focus areas: - Physical AI and robotics - Industrial IoT and smart manufacturing - Mobility and energy systems - Hardware + software products

Aging infrastructure, contamination, and rising water cuts are becoming frequent not just in developing regions but increasingly across Europe and the US. Kawo builds the first integrated home water station that gives households independence from these failing systems. It collects water from multiple sources (tap, rain, or well) purifies it with advanced reverse osmosis and cold plasma, stores it safely, and allows users to personalize taste and minerals to match their favorite premium bottled water brands. Kawo aims to redefine home water security by turning every household into its own private spring.
