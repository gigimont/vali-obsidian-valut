# Contact List

Master tracker for all customer-discovery-pipeline contacts. Sorted by status. Within each status group, most recent or upcoming activity first.

**Status conventions:**
- **Must contact** — identified target, no outreach yet
- **Scheduled** — meeting on the calendar, not yet held
- **Awaiting reply** — outreach sent, no response yet
- **Met — follow-up pending** — substantive conversation done; specific next action live
- **Met — warm** — substantive conversation done; relationship alive, no immediate action
- **Not in scope** — decided not to pursue (with note if revisit possible)

**Information architecture:**
- This file owns status, next step, last contact date, channel
- Individual person pages own background, history, conversation pointers, personal-level actions
- Interview notes own full conversation content
- Project-level actions live in [[Open Questions]], not on person pages

Contact details (email, phone) are deliberately not stored in this vault. Use Outlook, phone, or WhatsApp for those.
