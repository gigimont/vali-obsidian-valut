## Key Insights

- **Atomization of Bauermeister's existing archive is the Solco team's responsibility, not Bauermeister's.** This is the **main frontier of innovation** for the methodology: how to dissect and atomize tacit-laden documents into a queryable context layer. Bauermeister previously attempted a Python / vector-DB self-build ([[Interview 014 — Ulrich Bauermeister (Filigran, Germany)]]); his initiative confirms the demand but the methodology development sits with Solco.
- **Word native dictation as a data-security wedge.** Inserting a third-party LLM as transcription middleware introduces a data-exposure surface that founders / IT leads will flag. Word-direct keeps content on-device. If accuracy holds in German technical vocabulary, this becomes a reusable selling-point for any privacy-conscious Mittelstand engagement.
- **Recording cadence must start immediately — file structure precedes content capture.** Without a pre-designed folder / naming / tagging convention, the first weeks of recording produce uningestible mass. File-structure design is the first deliverable Marco owns.
- **Marco is the operational instantiation of Pattern 35.** Internal IT lead with authority + initiative + alignment on methodology. His leading the IT preparation is the methodology demonstrating Pattern 35 (three trust thresholds — strategic + technical + operational) in action at Filigran. Cross-reference [[Interview 022 — Marco Nortmeier (Filigran, Germany)]].
- **Kotter's 8 Steps becomes the C-level conversational vocabulary.** Translates Solco's organizational ask into a language that German Mittelstand CEOs (Wolf's father included) recognize as serious change-management practice rather than startup jargon.
- **Solco operates as service provider to Filigran, not as embedded co-builder.** Written legal posture matters: contracts, IP ownership, GDPR posture must be explicit. The pilot doubles as proof we can show buyer-side / PE during M&A wedge engagements that we run pilots with adult-grade legal infrastructure.
