## Where Things Stand

Week 7 is the validation tipping-point. The Mentor Day pitch event went well — Solco was one of the few startups with multi-mentor interest, the mentor / startup ratio worked out perfectly despite a mentor no-show, and the overall reception was positive. Two crystal-clear pieces of feedback emerged from every conversation: **(1)** Solco needs a CTO on the founding team, and **(2)** the monetization model — acquisition cost vs. pricing — needs sharpening. A third theme: the pitch is currently too broad and needs a tighter niche to lead with. Multiple mentors offered direct help, and at least one portfolio company is willing to be a second pilot. The Filigran pilot has kicked off operationally (Friday, 5-person team scope), and the application track to EWOR is being prepared with a warm channel to Florin Hubert. Company formation has moved forward via glaub.de.

## Key Discussions

### Pitch Event Feedback (high signal, converging)

- **CTO need flagged by every mentor.** Technical capability for a product as complex as Solco was the single most-questioned point. This confirms the strategic direction from earlier in the week (see [[Standup — 2026-05-25]] / [[Mentor Council Strategy]]): hiring a CTO is now non-negotiable and on the critical path.
- **Monetization strategy under question.** Acquisition cost vs. pricing model came up repeatedly. The current framing — services pricing in the €15–25K range with M&A ROI as the anchor ([[Interview 023 — Maciej Bogacz (Holon Translink, Poland)]]) — needs to be stress-tested against CAC realities, not just unit economics.
- **"Too broad, need a niche."** Despite the role-based ICP wedge already defined in [[ICP Definition]] (head-of-department / Planungsleiter at the ERP ceiling), the *first-impression positioning* still reads as too broad. Multiple mentors recommended leading with a single, named role.
- **Outcome positioning candidate: "technical directors."** Based on mentor feedback, the team is testing a rebrand of the lead-with persona from "Planungsleiter / head of department" to **"technical directors"** — sharper in English, more universally readable, and points cleanly at the Filigran Bautechnik / AWW use case. To be validated through interviews.
- **Multi-mentor interest** is itself a signal: Solco is one of the few startups in the cohort that pulled more than one mentor in. Treat this as conviction-evidence for the thesis, not as a vanity metric.
