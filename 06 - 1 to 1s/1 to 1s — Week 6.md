### Product Vision — Closed Loop System

- New framing crystallised: the product is a **closed loop knowledge system**, not just a knowledge capture tool
  - Business takes action → measures result → feeds outcome back into the brain → system improves continuously
  - Prevents knowledge base from becoming outdated when processes change
  - Accuracy compounds over time as the brain grows with real usage
- This closed loop is the differentiator in a crowded AI space — most tools capture once and go stale
- Demo strategy: show one specific use case end-to-end (construction paper digitization → action → measurement → feedback loop)
- Pitch framework: what we've done + why we're different (closed loop + SME succession focus = double differentiation)
