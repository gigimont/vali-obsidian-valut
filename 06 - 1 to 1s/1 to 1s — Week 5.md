## Product Development Strategy

### Three-pillar extraction approach (crystallised this week)
1. **Interview methodology (1/3):** Strategic questioning techniques — the "campfire" approach (Pattern 28). Life Book is the professional benchmark. Quality of interviews = quality of output.
2. **Computer use tracking (1/3):** Emails, Excel workflows, digital activity monitoring — shadows how experts actually work day-to-day.
3. **Document scanning (1/3):** Analog data processing — archives, printed drawings, certificates, local files.

### Key insight: interview methodology is the most uncertain pillar
- Pillars 2 and 3 are technically solvable with existing tools (screen recording, OCR, AI ingestion)
- Pillar 1 (how to extract tacit knowledge through conversation) is where the real moat lies
- Life Book contact is the most important lead for solving this
- Mistral AI identified as promising solution for document processing (pillar 3)

### Transition needed: concept → demonstration
- Need to move from high-level product descriptions to a concrete demo for future interviews
- "When can I buy this?" test — show something, gauge reaction
- Technical co-founder required to build the demo
