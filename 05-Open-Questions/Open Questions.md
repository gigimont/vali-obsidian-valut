## Answered Questions

### Answered from Customer & Buyer
- [x] Are search fund searchers (young CEOs post-acquisition) a viable customer segment? **YES.** Newton Campos (Interview 010) is a search fund GP who would pay for pre-packaged operational documentation. Operates at €10-25M EV.
- [x] Is "growth ambition in the successor" typical or rare? **TYPICAL.** Kevin (013) wanted to digitalize. Interview 001 successor wanted tenders and brand. Ariana (017) built e-commerce. Growth ambition is the norm.
- [x] What's the founder's actual position? **VARIES WIDELY.** Stefan (012): no plan, open. Marco (007): desperate. Joerg (015): burned out. Armin (017): actively planning. No single "founder position" — segmentation needed.
- [x] What is the precise digitalization-stage cutoff above which our value proposition breaks down? **NO CUTOFF.** Pattern 22 sharpened: BWB-Gruppe (fully digital) and AWW (ERP + analog) both have the same tacit knowledge bottleneck. Digital maturity does NOT eliminate the problem.
- [x] Does Lynka itself become a beta candidate? **NO.** Too large, competitor (Omnivisor) already engaged, wrong timing. John is a reference voice, not a customer.
- [x] Is "judgment framework capture" a better product name than "decision replication"? **YES — adopted.** Confirmed by Interview 011 ("chiavi di lettura") and Interview 019 (Lorenz's "soft vs hard knowledge" framing). Now the standard term in Problem Statement v5.
- [x] For an already-structured company, is "capturing decision-making knowledge" a differentiating position versus "documenting processes"? **YES.** Interview 011, Interview 013, Pattern 13 (decision-making gap). This IS the differentiator.
- [x] Does the engineering-archive use case have different competitive dynamics than the founder-decision-extraction case? **YES.** Pattern 34: different sequencing (Standardgrundlage-first vs campfire-first), different buyer persona (technical insider vs founder/successor), likely different pricing.

### Answered from Knowledge Extraction
- [x] Should we position with TWO explicit modes: prospective capture + retroactive extraction? **YES.** Newton (010) confirmed: prospective is uncontroversial, retroactive is partial (~60-70%). Two modes is the correct positioning.
- [x] Can decision replication give a successor the confidence to take over? **PARTIALLY.** Interview 011: "I give you the keys to reading, then you read the score." Product gives frameworks, not confidence directly. Confidence comes from using the frameworks.
- [x] Should the product have two distinct outputs? **YES — two outputs.** (1) Structured report for buyer/board. (2) Queryable knowledge base for incoming CEO/team.
- [x] Has anyone tried this before? **YES.** Life Book (story harvesting, now using AI). Management consultants (too expensive). ERP vendors (only digital layer). Nobody does exactly this at our price point for our ICP.
- [x] In a digitally mature SME, the untouched layer is marketing, HR, creative, IT, and parts of sales — is this the right scope? **BROADER.** Pattern 22 sharpened: the untouched layer is judgment, relationships, and exception-handling regardless of ERP maturity.

### Answered from Product & GTM
- [x] Should "pre-transformation documentation" become a third application? **YES — adopted in Problem Statement v5 as Pivot 13.**
- [x] What is the right framing for an internal Filigran exercise? **ANSWERED — co-development partner, delegation-first seminar approach ([[Standup — 2026-05-20]]).**

### Answered from Market & Economics
- [x] Is the M&A buyer a more viable paying customer than the SME owner? **YES, for first 6 months.** Antonio (008), Armin (017), Lorenz (019) all confirm. But Lorenz challenges M&A-only: always-on monitoring may be the retention play.
- [x] Is value erosion without succession quantifiable? **YES.** Vetreria (007): €550K → estimated €0 in 3 years. Concrete numbers exist.
- [x] Are most founder-centric SMEs profitable-and-stuck, or declining-and-stuck? **PROFITABLE-AND-STUCK.** Pattern 1 (profitability masks fragility). Most interviewees run profitable companies. The problem is complacency, not decline.
- [x] Pricing reality check: is "fairly humble amounts of money" with a 3-month proof window the actual pricing structure? **YES for initial engagement.** Pattern 24: owners expect progress at 3 months, confirmed value at 6. The 2-3 week framing was explicitly rejected by Lynka (006).
- [x] Has the 25%-of-savings success-fee model been tried and failed? **YES — RISKY.** Lynka (006): consultants found €500K/year savings, Lynka never adopted them. Success-fee models fail when adoption fails. Flat fee is safer.
- [x] Does the "internal champion" requirement shrink or expand our addressable market? **SHRINKS, but necessary.** Pattern 6 confirmed: without internal champion, initiatives die. Accept the constraint and screen for it.
