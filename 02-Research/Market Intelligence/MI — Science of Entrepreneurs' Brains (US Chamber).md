## What This Says

A HEC Liège / CHU Liège study using resting-state fMRI found that **serial entrepreneurs have stronger neural connections between two brain regions** that non-entrepreneurs do not:
- **Right insula** — associated with flexible thinking
- **Anterior prefrontal cortex** — associated with new decision-making

This unique wiring enables serial entrepreneurs to **switch between exploiting current opportunities and exploring new ideas** — the balance that drives startup success. The study frames this as trainable, not purely innate.

## Key Insights Relevant to Solco

### 1. The founder's judgment IS their brain — and it's not transferable biologically

The right insula ↔ anterior prefrontal cortex connection enables the founder to simultaneously exploit what works now while exploring what comes next. This is 30 years of tuned neural pathways built through decisions, failures, and pattern recognition. When the founder leaves, this wiring stays with them.

- **Solco cross-reference:** This is the deepest "why" for what we do. Succession fails not because knowledge wasn't documented — it fails because the successor's brain hasn't built the same neural architecture yet. Solco doesn't replace 30 years of brain-building, but it externalises the OUTPUT of that architecture: the decision patterns, the judgment frameworks, the "I've seen this before" recognition. Capture the output; let the successor build their own wiring over time with a head start.

### 2. Cognitive flexibility = the asset that disappears

The right insula connection enables flexible thinking — switching mental models rapidly. Founders navigate ambiguity, read context, and make judgment calls that no SOP can encode. This is what successor interviews consistently describe as missing: not the process, but the judgment.

- **Solco cross-reference:** "Chiavi di lettura" (judgment frameworks) from Interview 011 — Dairy Chemicals Commercial. The commercial director couldn't teach the successor WHAT to decide — only HOW to think about the decision space. Our extraction methodology must go beyond process documentation to capture the cognitive heuristics: "when you see X, think about Y before deciding." That's the right insula output made explicit.

### 3. "Cognitive flexibility can be trained" = the case for a knowledge base as training tool

The article notes that even without the natural right insula wiring, the entrepreneurial mindset can be developed. Training methods include: expecting change, recognizing patterns, accepting risks, embracing failure.

- **Solco cross-reference:** The successor's 2-year onboarding is exactly this cognitive training process — they're building the neural architecture through experience. Solco accelerates it by giving them the founder's accumulated pattern library to train against. Instead of learning from zero, they train against a rich repository of "here's how the founder saw this situation." Solco is the cognitive training curriculum built from the founder's brain.
