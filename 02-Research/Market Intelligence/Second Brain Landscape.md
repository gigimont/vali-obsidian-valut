## Why This Matters for Solcus

We are building a second brain for SMEs — but we are not the only ones thinking about this problem. Consumer AI companies (Mem, Notion AI, Rewind), enterprise knowledge platforms (Guru, Tettra, Glean), and academic researchers are all converging on the same insight: AI can now understand context well enough to make unstructured knowledge usable without manual organisation.

Understanding this landscape helps us:
1. Identify what's been tried and what failed
2. Find methodologies and technical approaches we can adopt
3. Position Solcus distinctly (we extract from PEOPLE, not from existing digital data)
4. Anticipate where the technology is heading

## Key Themes Across the Landscape

### 1. The organisation problem is dead
Traditional knowledge bases require the CREATOR to organise information (folders, tags, categories). This never works because: the creator's structure makes sense to them and nobody else, information belongs in multiple categories simultaneously, and maintenance is unsustainable. AI eliminates this by matching information to CONTEXT at retrieval time, not at creation time.
- **Solcus relevance:** Our product captures knowledge through stories and observation — inherently unstructured. The AI must organise it automatically. We should never ask SME employees to categorise their knowledge.

### 2. Personalisation is the unlock
"The same search for different people should yield different results." A successor asking "how do we handle late-paying clients?" needs different context than the CFO asking the same question. The AI must model WHO is asking and adapt.
- **Solcus relevance:** Our queryable knowledge base must be role-aware. The Planungsleiter gets technical answers. The successor gets strategic context. Same data, different presentation.

### 3. Beyond search → reasoning
The value of a second brain goes beyond "find the document." It extends to: help me think through this problem, surface connections I wouldn't see, proactively alert me when something is relevant. This is the "personal EA" vision.
- **Solcus relevance:** Our deliverable should not just be a searchable archive. It should be a reasoning partner — "given what we know about how the founder handled client X, here's how you might approach this situation."

### 4. Cost is collapsing
Compute costs for running LLMs are dropping by an order of magnitude every several months. What costs €10 today will cost €1 next year and €0.10 the year after.
- **Solcus relevance:** Our service model (high-touch, €15-25K per engagement) is viable now. But the long-term play is a continuously updated knowledge system at SaaS pricing. The cost curve makes this inevitable.

### 5. The "hidden reinvention" problem
Workers don't just lose time searching — they lose time RECREATING work that already exists somewhere. This is invisible because they don't know what they don't know.
- **Solcus relevance:** This is exactly the Planungsleiter bottleneck. The 130 planners don't ask the expert because they don't know what to ask — they just do the work themselves, slower and worse. Our product makes the "I didn't know to ask" problem visible.
