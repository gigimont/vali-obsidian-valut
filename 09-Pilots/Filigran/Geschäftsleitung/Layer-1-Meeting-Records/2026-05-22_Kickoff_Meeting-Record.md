## 5. Decisions made

- **Start an internal Filigran pilot using the SOLCO methodology.** Who: Wolf + Filigran leadership. Rationale: the approach is validated externally (~30 interviews); now apply it inward. Supersedes: the "should we even do this" question.
- **Bautechnik is the first concrete build; Produktionsplanung is the priority second target.** Rationale: Bauermeister already has the archive and prior attempt; Produktionsplanung is the biggest lever but more complex. 
- **Begin forward-capture recording immediately, defer structuring.** Rationale: stop ongoing knowledge loss now; the sorting method can be solved later.
- **Each domain needs its own separate "brain."** Rationale: atomizing to a granularity an AI can use is domain-specific and complex; Planungsleitung/Bautechnik is a better starting point than Produktionsplanung because more of it is rule/standard-driven.
